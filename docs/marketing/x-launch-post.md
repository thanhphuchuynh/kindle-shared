## English Thread

1/ I built Kindle Share, a tiny macOS utility for sending books to Kindle over local Wi-Fi.

The idea is simple: your Mac already has the files, your Kindle is already nearby, so the transfer should not need a cloud account or USB cable.

2/ How it works:

Choose a books folder on Mac.
Start sharing.
Open the local URL in the Kindle browser.
Tap a book to download.

That is the whole workflow.

3/ This is not trying to replace Calibre.

Calibre is powerful and library-centric. Kindle Share is intentionally smaller: a quick local book drop for PDF, EPUB, MOBI, AZW, and AZW3 files.

4/ Why I wanted it:

Send to Kindle is convenient, but cloud-based.
USB works, but needs a cable.
Calibre works, but can be more than I need.

Kindle Share sits in the middle: local, tiny, direct.

5/ It is early and open source. Next ideas: QR code, Bonjour local address, auto-refresh folders, and a better E-ink web page.

Try it here:
https://github.com/thanhphuchuynh/kindle-shared
